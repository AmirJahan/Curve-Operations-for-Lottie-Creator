Curve Ops brings Illustrator-style pathfinder tools to LottieFiles Creator. Select two or more shapes and choose Union, Subtract, Intersect or Exclude to combine them into a single layer, or use Blend to fuse separate shapes with a smooth, organic bridge. Blend arcs meet each outline tangentially, so every connection point has perfectly aligned handles and no visible creases. A live preview shows the exact result before you apply it, and adjustable arc radius and tolerance controls let you shape waists or bulges and balance precision against point count. Results come back as efficient bezier curves, styled to match your originals.
